Texts the official translations left in English

`Untranslated_in_FR_DE_IT_ES.csv` lists every text (with words) that the official French, German,
Italian and Spanish versions all kept identical to the English one, from the `Default_all_*` CSVs of
this folder. A text no official translation touched is unused in game or meant to stay as it is,
so a new translation can skip it.

Columns: Type, Section (field name, monster, kernel/mngrp section...), Text id, Kind (`sentence`:
3+ words, quotes or final punctuation; `short`: names and labels), English text.

| Type | Texts | Identical in all four: sentences | short |
|---|---|---|---|
| field | 20,887 | 548 | 29 |
| world | 122 | 0 | 0 |
| battle | 510 | 0 | 9 |
| kernel | 1,278 | 0 | 16 |
| mngrp | 2,542 | 0 | 22 |
| namedic | 32 | 0 | 7 |
| exe | 296 | 0 | 13 |

What they are:

- Debug fields: `test`, `test1`-`test14`, `testno` (about 540 texts), never reached in game.
- `gfhtl1`: an unused hotel field ("1F is the restaurant, 2F is the hotel..."), cut content.
- "Test" placeholder lines in real fields: `bghall1b`, `fewor2`, `rghang11`, `rghang2`, `tvglen3`.
- `fepic1`: the Latin inscription "VIVIDARIUM ET INTERVIGILIUM ET VIATOR", kept on purpose.
- Names kept in every language: characters (kernel, Wedge, Biggs, Edea...), some card names (exe),
  places (namedic), and a few menu labels (mngrp `tkmnmes2`/`tkmnmes3`, `TextBox05`).

Made by comparing the rows of each `Default_all_*_EN.csv` with the same rows (same keys; monsters by
index, since their names are translated) of the FR, DE, IT and ES files.
